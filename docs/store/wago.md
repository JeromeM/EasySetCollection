# Easy Set Collection

An addon for transmog set collectors. It lists every set, shows what you are
still missing, tells you where each piece comes from, and takes you there.

## Features

- **Every set, and 1,720 more**: the sets from the game's journal, plus all the
  ones it doesn't list (dungeon recolors, world sets, old PvP sets, event gear)
  on their own tab. New ones are added automatically every week.
- **Where each piece comes from**: boss and instance with the difficulty, quest
  name, or vendor with the price. No more tab hopping to a website.
- **Guide me**: sets a waypoint and shows a 3D arrow to the right place. Click a
  single piece first and it takes you to that one. With FarstriderLib installed,
  you get step-by-step routing with hearthstone and teleport buttons.
- **Suggest**: finds the closest set you can still farm this week and takes you
  there. It skips what is locked and the bosses you already killed.
- **Weekly lockouts**: sets you can no longer loot this week are marked, and you
  can hide them.
- **In-instance helper**: when you enter a dungeon or a raid that still drops
  pieces you need, it tells you which boss has what.
- **Preview**: your character wearing the set, in the window. Full set, or only
  the pieces you own.
- **Filters and search**: what you own, expansion, content type, class, faction,
  unobtainable sets, and more.
- **Notifications**: a small popup when you collect a piece, with the new
  progress of the set.

Bosses, instances, items and difficulties are read live from the game, so they
always show up in your language. The addon's own text is in English and French
for now. **If you would like it in your language, I am looking for
translators** — say hello on GitHub:
https://github.com/JeromeM/EasySetCollection/issues

## Getting started

Type `/esc` or click the minimap button. The setup window will walk you through
the options on your first login, and a short tour points out the main buttons.

## Optional dependency

FarstriderLib adds step-by-step travel routes to "Guide me".
